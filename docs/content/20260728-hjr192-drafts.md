Share post draft (assembled with Hook 1, ~130 words):

1977. Write that number down before you comment 'FREE' on anything.

HJR-192 is a real law from 1933. Here's what it actually says: it voided contracts that demanded payment in gold, and it said a debt counts as paid once you pay it in regular U.S. currency. That's the whole thing. No bill cancellation, no secret government account.

The part people quote, 31 U.S.C. § 5118(d)(2), doesn't even apply anymore — the statute itself says it does not apply to any obligation issued after October 27, 1977. Your credit card, your car loan, your mortgage: all obligations issued after that date, all outside this law.

People who filed on this theory anyway didn't get their bills cancelled. One got 135 months in federal prison.

Follow for the next one we break down.
